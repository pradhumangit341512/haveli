Heritage Hotel in Pratap Nagar, Jaipur

Rising majestically in Pratap Nagar on Tonk Road, The Ummed Haveli is a five-story architectural marvel inspired by Rajasthan's timeless haveli tradition. Every arched window, carved balustrade, and hand-painted detail reflects the artistry of master Rajasthani craftsmen.


Just 2 kilometres from Jaipur International Airport, our 21 luxury rooms offer the perfect confluence of heritage aesthetics and five-star modern comfort. Our rooftop restaurant frames panoramic views of the airport runway against the dramatic silhouette of the Aravallis — making us the best hotel near Jaipur airport for discerning travelers.

Rajasthan Culture, Festivals & Traditions

Rajasthan is a living epic of colour, music, and devotion. Time your visit to Jaipur with these extraordinary celebrations and cultural experiences.

March

Holi — Festival of Colours

Jaipur erupts in a riot of gulal, folk music, and celebration. Experience the world's most vibrant festival from our rooftop.
October-November

Diwali — Festival of Lights

The Pink City transforms into a golden constellation. Thousands of diyas illuminate every haveli and palace in Jaipur.
Living Tradition

UNESCO-recognized Rajasthani folk dances performed at our cultural evenings. Centuries-old traditions come alive.
Year-Round

Visit artisan workshops just 2 km from our hotel. Witness the 500-year-old hand block printing textile art of Sanganer.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is The Ummed Haveli from Jaipur Airport?
The Ummed Haveli is just 2 km (5 minutes drive) from Jaipur International Airport (JAI). We offer complimentary airport pickup for all guests. Our rooftop restaurant offers direct views of the airport runway and Aravalli hills.
What is the room rate at The Ummed Haveli?
Room rates start from Rs 5,000 per night for the Royal Deluxe Room. The Maharaja Heritage Suite starts at Rs 8,000 and the Rajputana Family Suite from Rs 10,000. All rates include complimentary breakfast, Wi-Fi, and airport transfer.
Does The Ummed Haveli have a rooftop restaurant?
Yes, our signature rooftop restaurant offers panoramic views of Jaipur Airport and the Aravalli hills. We serve authentic Rajasthani cuisine including Dal Baati Churma and Laal Maas, along with North Indian and multi-cuisine options. Open daily for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
Is The Ummed Haveli suitable for wedding guest bookings?
Absolutely. We specialize in wedding guest group bookings with block rates for 10-50+ rooms. We offer group airport transfers, decorated rooms, and coordination with nearby wedding venues in South Jaipur. Peak wedding season is October to February.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out available on request. We accommodate early morning airport arrivals with complimentary waiting lounge access.
What tourist attractions are near the hotel?
Patrika Gate (5 km), Hawa Mahal (12 km), City Palace (13 km), Jal Mahal (16 km), Amber Fort (22 km), Sanganer Block Printing workshops (2 km), and Sitapura EPIP business zone (4 km). Our travel desk arranges daily sightseeing tours.
Does the hotel offer free Wi-Fi and parking?
Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi in all rooms and public areas. Free secure parking for all guests. Dedicated work desks in every room for business travelers.
Is the hotel good for corporate and business stays?
Yes, we offer corporate rate packages for travelers visiting Sitapura EPIP, JECC Convention Centre, and Jaipur's southern business corridor. Monthly billing, long-stay discounts, and meeting space available.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in for most room types. Non-refundable rates available at discounted prices. Group bookings have specific terms discussed during reservation.
How can I book a room?
Book directly on our website for the best rates, or call +91 9XXXXXXXXX, or WhatsApp us for instant confirmation. Also available on MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, Goibibo, Agoda, and Google Hotel Search.
